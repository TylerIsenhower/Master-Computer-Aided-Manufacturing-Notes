# Form and Shape Measurement Systems

#mmet380 #ch8 #concept

## In Plain English

Sometimes, we need to measure the overall "vibe" or shape of a part rather than just one dimension. **3D Scanners** are the ultimate tool for this—they "digitize" the part by taking millions of measurements in seconds and stitching them into a 3D model. For round parts (like cylinders), we use a **Roundness System**. This spins the part on a perfectly straight axis while a sensitive needle tracks the surface. It tells you exactly how much the part "wobbles" or is "out-of-round."

## 1. Three-Dimensional (3D) Scanning
- **Principle:** Uses cameras and lasers to take images from all angles and "stiches" them together using software.
- **Capabilities:** Rapidly measures length, diameter, volume, and surface area. 
- **Application:** Ideal for **Reverse Engineering**—creating a digital model from a physical part.

## 2. Roundness Measurement Systems
- **Principle:** The part is rotated on a high-precision vertical axis. A sensitive probe tracks the radial deviation of the surface.
- **GD&T Features Measured:**
    - Roundness (Circularity)
    - Cylindricity
    - Concentricity
    - Runout and Total Runout
    - Parallelism (to the axis)

## Why specialized systems?
While a CMM *can* measure roundness, a specialized Roundness System is much more accurate for rotating features because its spindle is built to be "mathematically perfect" in its rotation.
