# Metrology and Measuring Devices

#mmet380 #ch7 #concept

## In Plain English

GD&T symbols on a drawing are just theories until someone in a lab verifies them. This process is **Metrology**. To measure a part correctly, you must first "freeze" it in space so it doesn't move. Every object in the world has **6 Degrees of Freedom** (it can move in 3 directions and spin in 3 directions). By clamping the part against **Datums** (like a flat granite table and two side rails), you remove these degrees of freedom one by one until the part is perfectly locked for measurement.

## Degrees of Freedom (DOF)

Any unconstrained part in space has 6 DOF:
- **Translation:** 3 directions (X, Y, Z).
- **Rotation:** 3 directions (Roll, Pitch, Yaw).

## Restricting Motion with Datums

| Datum Level | Restriction |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Primary** | Restricts 3 DOF (1 Translation, 2 Rotation). Usually a large flat surface. |
| **Secondary**| Restricts 2 DOF (1 Translation, 1 Rotation). Usually an edge. |
| **Tertiary** | Restricts 1 DOF (Final Translation). Usually a second edge or a point. |

## Metrology Equipment

- **Dial Indicators:** Used to measure straightness, flatness, and runout. Can be push-pull or rotate probe types.
- **Laser Displacement Sensors:** Non-contact sensors that use light to measure tiny deviations in surface height.
- **V-Blocks:** Used to hold cylindrical parts securely during measurement.
- **Angle Blocks / Plug Gages:** Standardized references used to verify angles or hole sizes.
